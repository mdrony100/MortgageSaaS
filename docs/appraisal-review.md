## Lease/unit/rent workflow
Extract address, unit, parties, monthly rent, dates, visible signature, continuation, concessions, utilities and amendments. A candidate requires a unique matching reported property address and explicit unique unit. Conflicts/unknown matching require human evidence; original leases/amendments never overwrite each other.

Per unit keep loan occupancy separate from inspection/current/lease status. Occupied asks current executed lease/amendments under the selected path. Expired/MTM asks continuation/receipts, never assumes renewal. Confirmed vacancy creates distinct program-permission, market-rent-use and supporting-documentation questions, identifying partial/full and refinance path. STR stays a separate method/evidence path without automatic annualization. Missing lease is not vacant.

Compare extracted lease/rentroll/appraisal rents and explicit reviewer selections side by side. Qualifying rent needs basis+source+confirmation. No automatic higher/lower/market/cap/renewal/STR-LTR/ADU substitution. Unknown is not0; confirmed0 is deliberate. Calculations remain provisional with missing policy/evidence.

## Decisions, revisions, reports
Notes, corrections and finding statuses append actor/time/reason. Reviewed/resolved/follow-up/reopened/dismissed are tracking, not lender condition clearance. Original observation and pinned checklist remain.

Revision links are same-file, preserve originals/reports, compare added/removed/changed field identity/content rather than page number alone, and show prior/proposed sources. They never replace confirmed calculation inputs or auto-resolve findings. Explicitly confirmed input updates create new calculations.

Issue real PDF/XLSX with open findings; immutable content and actual private file bytes/version/SHA remain. Source/calculation/report/checklist/input versions and coverage/provisional status are included. No underwriting sign-off or loan outcome is provided.

Live vision accuracy and real client-form/template acceptance remain unverified; provider mocks/synthetic documents are clearly labeled.
