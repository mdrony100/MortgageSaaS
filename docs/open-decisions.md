# Open decisions requiring product / lender approval

1. Final investor definitions, document eligibility and versioned rulesets. No Fannie Mae or investor approval is asserted.
2. NSF **event versus fee** counting, reversals and overdraft-day conventions.
3. Transfer matching amount/date tolerance and multi-account ambiguity.
4. Business sustainability thresholds, concentrations, reserve definitions and decision semantics.
5. Document-retention period, legal hold, purge authorization, regional residency, encryption and restore policy.
6. Final shared-file permission matrix, processor rights, viewer notes and wide admin-grant/ownership-transfer process.
7. Fraud vendor selection (Ocrolus Detect vs MoneyThumb Thumbprint or another approved option), supported document types, original-evidence requirements, false-positive policy and vendor contracts.
8. Approved model(s), extraction/classification acceptance targets, evaluation datasets, monitoring and human escalation.
9. Approved lender workbook template/mapping; 24-row/12-selector and overflow/exclusions conventions require acceptance.
10. Appraisal/rent form/occupancy policies, photo expectations, report-release blocking findings, revision rules and DSCR/PITIA/LTV definitions.
11. Sample pricing and final plan/entitlement semantics; billing provider/checkout intentionally deferred.
12. Antivirus/sandbox vendor, scan-verdict retention, quarantined-original release policy and incident response before any real document ingestion.
13. Production domains/hosting, HTTPS/session settings, secrets manager, queues/alerts/backups, SMTP/S3 verification, accessibility, browser coverage and penetration-test ownership. No deployment is included in Phase 1.
